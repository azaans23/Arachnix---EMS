# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HR admins, managers, and ops staff who manage employees, roles, and access during weekday work hours. Primary job: sign in quickly, find people, register or update employees, and complete admin tasks without friction.

## Product Purpose

Arachnix EMS is an internal employee management tool. Success means clear workflows, trustworthy data presentation, and a UI that feels like a serious ops product rather than a marketing site or template dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Precise, industrial-tech, confident. Voice is direct and calm. Emotion: controlled competence, not playful or decorative.

## Anti-references

- Warm cream + terracotta "AI SaaS" palettes
- Purple-to-indigo gradient themes
- Soft glassmorphism and glow stacks
- Generic Inter-only marketing landing aesthetics
- Hero-metric dashboard clichés and identical icon card grids

## Design Principles

1. **Tool first** — every screen serves a workflow; decoration never competes with the task.
2. **Brand through restraint** — black, silver, and precise geometry carry identity; accents stay sparse.
3. **Stripe-grade clarity** — sharp hierarchy, generous whitespace, confident type, purposeful motion.
4. **Light by default** — daylight office readability; dark mode as a deliberate alternate, not the personality.
5. **Earn every pixel** — no filler stats, no ornamental chrome, no AI-template patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast on text and controls. Support keyboard focus, reduced motion, and clear error states near the field that failed.
